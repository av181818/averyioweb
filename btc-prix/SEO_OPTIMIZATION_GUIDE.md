## 📊 SEO Monitoring

### Key Metrics to Track
1. **Impressions** - How often your site appears in search
2. **Click-through rate (CTR)** - Percentage clicking from search
3. **Average position** - Your ranking in search results
4. **Bounce rate** - Users leaving without interaction
5. **Time on page** - User engagement indicator
6. **Backlinks** - Links pointing to your site
