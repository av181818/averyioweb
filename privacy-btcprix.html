BTCPRIX

Privacy Policy — Effective August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

BTCPRIX is a free web app by averyio, at averyio.net/btc-prix, that shows the live Bitcoin price. This policy explains what the page stores, what it sends, and to whom — which, in short, is: nothing about you to us. averyio runs no server behind this app and holds no data about its users.

2. What Is Stored in Your Browser

One thing, and only on your device, in your browser's local storage: your theme choice (light or dark). It is never transmitted anywhere. There is no account, no sign-up and no identifier of any kind, and averyio never sets a cookie.

3. Third-Party Services

The page runs in your browser, so your browser contacts a small number of third-party servers to make it work. Each sees the standard information any web request carries (your IP address, browser type and the resource asked for), and each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Binance (stream.binance.com) supplies the live price over a streaming connection that stays open while the page is. No account and no identifier is involved; the connection carries only price data one way.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces and Tailwind CDN serves the page's styling library.
  • TradingView hosts the banner image shown on the page, so your browser requests that image from TradingView's server on load.
  • X is reached only if you choose to tap Share, which opens it in a new tab.

averyio does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks or any service that profiles visitors. Nothing on this page identifies you to averyio.

4. The TradingView Affiliate Link

The page carries a link to TradingView that is an affiliate link: if you follow it and later sign up, averyio may receive a commission from TradingView, at no cost to you. This is disclosed beside the link on the page itself. Following it takes you to tradingview.com, where TradingView's own privacy policy applies; the affiliate reference travels in the link's address, and averyio receives no personal information about you from TradingView as a result. Nothing is set on averyio.net by this link, and it does nothing until you choose to click it.

5. Not Financial Advice

BTCPRIX shows a market price for information and entertainment only. It is not investment advice, and averyio accepts no liability for any decision made on the strength of it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the page ever changes what it stores or whom it contacts, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date at the top of the page. The current version is always at this address.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or the app: averyio.net/contact.
