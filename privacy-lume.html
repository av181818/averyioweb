Lume

Privacy Policy — Effective August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Lume is a task manager developed by averyio. We built Lume to be a private app — your tasks, categories and progress belong to you, they are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, and we deliberately do not collect, store or share your personal information. This policy explains exactly what happens with your data.

2. Information Collection and Use

Lume does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no app-level account, no sign-up, no login. Every task, category, completion, point total, rank and streak you accumulate is stored locally on your device.

Your data is also mirrored through your own private iCloud database (see section 6). It is never transmitted to a server operated by averyio, and we have no ability to read it.

3. Payment

Lume is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, and every feature is included. The transaction is processed exclusively by Apple; averyio does not see, receive, or store your payment credentials at any point. (Earlier versions offered a "Lume Pro" in-app purchase; it no longer exists, and everything it unlocked is now included for everyone.)

4. Analytics and Usage Data

Lume does not include any third-party analytics SDK, telemetry library, or in-app tracking. The App Store privacy nutrition label declares "Data Not Collected", and that is literally the case.

Apple may, separately and only with your consent, provide developers with anonymous, aggregated metrics through App Store Connect — such as download counts, crash reports, and session counts. These reports contain no personally identifiable information and cannot be linked back to an individual user. You can opt out of sharing this data with developers at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Lume does not display advertisements, does not use ad networks, and does not embed third-party tracking or marketing tools. The only external service Lume relies on is Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) for sync, and Apple's StoreKit for processing the in-app purchase — both of which are governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

6. iCloud Sync and Data Storage

iCloud sync is on by default. Your tasks, categories and progress are mirrored through your personal iCloud private database — bound to your own Apple ID — so the same data is available across your devices and survives losing, replacing or resetting a device. This is the same mechanism Apple's own apps use.

The private database is, by design:

  • Inaccessible to averyio — we cannot view, query, or recover its contents.
  • Inaccessible to Apple's staff — Apple encrypts it in transit and at rest.
  • Free of any app-level account or identifier — sync uses your Apple ID directly.

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in the iPhone Settings app, using the same iCloud control iOS provides for every app. There is no switch inside Lume itself. Lume carries on working normally with sync off — your data simply stays on the device. If you are not signed in to iCloud, sync does nothing and Lume runs entirely locally.

Turning sync off does not delete anything: data already on a device stays on that device, and the copy already in your iCloud stays in your iCloud until you remove it. If you use the in-app "Wipe all data" option while sync is active, the deletion is mirrored to iCloud and to any other device signed into the same Apple ID — there is no separate iCloud backup. For a portable copy of your data you can keep independent of iCloud, use the Export feature in Settings > Backup.

7. Children's Privacy

Lume is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or to clarify our practices. When we do, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or about how Lume handles your data, please visit our Contact Us page.
